## Observations (reconstructed from SKILL.md v1.0 references)

- Shell: one `components/AppShell.tsx` with sticky header, view tabs and a centered column; `.dash-columns` in `app/globals.css` widens to about 1240 px at 1024 px and up.
- Header: wordmark, place picker, compact status, account control (`components/Header.tsx`, `Header.module.css`).
- Account menu: `components/AccountMenu.tsx` follows the WAI-ARIA menu button pattern with `lib/roving-focus.ts` and `lib/use-outside-click.ts`; tested in `tests/harness/account-menu.spec.ts`.
- Settings: one opener (`lib/open-settings.ts`, `components/SettingsHost.tsx`), a sectioned dialog (`components/PreferencesModal.tsx`, `components/settings/SettingsTabs.tsx`, `SettingsCard.tsx`), draft model with `isSettingsDraftDirty` in `lib/settings.ts`.
- States: `components/dashboard/ActivityEmptyState.tsx`, `components/Skeleton.tsx`, `lib/cached-account.ts`, `AccountLoadError` in `components/AccountStatus.tsx`, `components/ErrorBoundary.tsx`, `components/ForecastNotice.tsx`, `components/AppNotice.tsx`, `syncFailed` in `messages/dashboard.ts`.
- Onboarding: separate 3-step flow, then one dismissible tip per visit (`lib/tips.ts`).
- Dialogs: native `<dialog>` primitive `components/Dialog.tsx` with `lib/useBodyScrollLock.ts`; `components/BottomSheet.tsx`.
- Tokens: `:root` and `.dark` in `app/globals.css`, contrast unit test `lib/design-tokens.test.ts`, font in `app/fonts.ts`, theme boot in `components/ThemeInit.tsx` and `lib/theme.ts`.
- PWA: `public/manifest.webmanifest` with `tests/manifest.test.ts`, `scripts/build-sw.mjs`, `components/InstallPrompt.tsx`, `lib/install-prompt.ts`, `components/UpdateNotice.tsx`.
- Harness: `playwright.harness.config.ts`, `tests/harness/capture.ts`, `tests/harness/freeze.ts`, `tests/harness/dashboard-states.spec.ts`; e2e in `tests/e2e/signed-in/preferences.e2e.ts`, `tests/e2e/offline.spec.ts`.
- Performance: commit `e0ed060` (2026-10-25) loads Firebase on demand and defers dialogs, LCP 4.4 s to 2.3 s on `/app/` (added 2026-11-04 by the v1.1 study from `git log`).
