# Nathan's voice (draft)

Last updated: 2026-11-02.
Sources: 965 of Nathan's messages to coding agents (2026-10-21 to 2026-11-02) and four published n8 Notions posts on n8builds.dev.
The chat messages show how he thinks and what he values; the posts show his public writing, though they were likely drafted with AI help.
Update this file from his own edits to drafts and from his X posts once he posts regularly.

## Summary

Builder-first, practical and collaborative.
He leads with the point, backs it with real numbers, and keeps a slightly rough, hands-on edge.
He cares about what ships and how it looks, and prefers simple over clever.

## Published writing

- Open with a one-line thesis, then the stakes: "Building in public is uncomfortable, and that is exactly why it works."
- Land points with short declaratives: "Easy trade." "That asymmetry is the whole game now."
- Back claims with named studies and real numbers, then give the fair caveat ("Two caveats worth knowing").
- First person, plain words, "here is the trade as I see it". No hype adjectives.
- Practical close: what a builder should actually do next.

## How he talks (for chat-like copy and replies)

- Mostly lowercase and quick; collaborative "we" and "let's".
- Short check-ins ("how are we doing", "go ahead and merge it") and long think-out-loud brain dumps that mix decisions, questions and ideas.
- Asks for judgment directly ("what do you think?", "thoughts?") and says plainly when something is confusing.
- Taste notes come with an approximate target ("revert it about 85% to what it was").

## Do

- Lead with the point; keep sentences short.
- Use real numbers, name the source, include the caveat.
- Talk like a builder showing his work: what was tried, what happened, what changed.

## Don't

- No em dashes.
- No hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no corporate voice, no filler.
- Don't polish away the practical edge.
